Is Micro1 Legit?

Short answer from my own experience: yes. Here's what that's based on, and the caveats.

My experience

  • I've done paid work there, and payments have been very reliable. I've never had an issue.
  • Training, assessments and quizzes inside a project are paid once you've onboarded. The qualifying assessments before that aren't.
  • The work is real AI training work for the big AI companies, and teams are well organised with clear communication.
  • The AI interview is thorough but not hard if you prepare. See how to prepare.

The caveats

  • Acceptance isn't guaranteed. You're matched to specific roles, so fit matters.
  • Timing varies. For me it was one week to one month from applying to first paid work.
  • Avoid scams: apply only through Micro1's own site, never pay anyone for a "guaranteed" role, and be wary of people messaging you claiming to be Micro1 recruiters.

That's my experience, not a guarantee for every role or country.

Remote AI Training Jobs for Physicians & Medical Professionals

AI companies pay physicians and medical professionals to help train their models, remotely and around your clinical schedule. I work on both platforms (I'm not a physician, so I can speak to the process, not the medical work).

What the work involves

Medical roles typically involve reviewing AI answers to clinical questions, writing realistic medical scenarios, and judging whether an output meets professional standards. Rates vary widely by specialty and licence; check each posting.

How to get in

  • Both platforms start with an AI interview. Mercor's felt slightly easier to me. See how to prepare.
  • On Mercor, take as many assessments as you can. Many offers come without an interview. See Mercor assessments.
  • Apply to both. Mercor got me into a role faster; Micro1 has more roles, added daily.

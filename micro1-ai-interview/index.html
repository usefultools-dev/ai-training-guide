How to Pass the Micro1 AI Interview

Notes from someone who passed it and works on Micro1 and Mercor. No hype, no leaked questions, just what helps.

What the interview is like

Micro1 interviews you with an AI interviewer, not a person. In my experience it was thorough, and the questions were clearly tied to the role I applied for. It wasn't hard, but it rewarded preparation. Mercor also uses an AI interview; theirs felt slightly easier to me.

How I'd prepare

  1. Read the role posting carefully. The field, the skills listed and the tasks described are your study guide.
  2. Review your fundamentals. Brush up on the core concepts the role mentions, as if explaining them to a smart beginner.
  3. Practise explaining your reasoning out loud. AI training work is about judgement: why one answer is better than another.
  4. Set up a quiet space. Good internet, no interruptions; test your mic and camera.
  5. Be specific and honest. Use real examples from your own work and don't overstate experience.

This is general preparation advice, not inside information. Never share or look for interview or assessment questions; both platforms forbid it.

After the interview

From applying to first paid work took me between one week and one month on Micro1, depending on the role. Micro1 has a lot of roles, constantly hiring, with new ones added daily, so it's worth applying to several that fit you.

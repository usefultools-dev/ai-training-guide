Mercor Assessments: Getting Offers Without an Interview

The single most useful thing I've learned on Mercor, from working there as a contractor.

Take as many assessments as you can

Mercor has many assessments that qualify you for roles automatically. The more you take, the better your chances, and Mercor gives many offers this way without an interview.

Do the mini certifications

Mercor also offers short courses and mini certifications, for example in prompt engineering and agentic training, that qualify you for work.

What's paid and what isn't

The qualifying assessments you take before joining a project aren't paid. Once you've onboarded to a project, training, assessments and quizzes inside that project are paid. It's the same on Micro1.

How fast it can be

Mercor got me into a role in under a week. Pay has been about the same as Micro1 for me, and payments have been very reliable.

FAQ

Can you get a Mercor offer without an interview?

Yes. Many offers come from Mercor's assessments, which qualify you for roles automatically without an interview.

Are Mercor assessments paid?

Qualifying assessments before a project aren't paid. Training and assessments inside a project, once you've onboarded, are paid.

What are Mercor mini certifications?

Short courses, for example in prompt engineering and agentic training, that qualify you for work.

How long does Mercor take to get a role?

In my case, under a week.

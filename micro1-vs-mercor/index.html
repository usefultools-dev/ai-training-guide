Micro1 vs Mercor: Which Is Better?

I work on both. In short: Micro1 has more roles, added daily; Mercor gets you into a role faster. Apply to both.

Side by side, from my experience

  • Interview: both use an AI interviewer. Mercor's felt slightly easier.
  • Speed: Mercor got me into a role in under a week. Micro1 took one week to one month.
  • Number of roles: Micro1 has more, constantly hiring, with new roles added daily.
  • Getting offers: on Mercor, assessments qualify you automatically and many offers come without an interview.
  • Pay: about the same on both for me. Rates are shown on each posting.
  • Payments: very reliable on both, never an issue.
  • What's paid: on both, training and assessments are paid only inside a project, once you've onboarded.
  • Clients: both do AI training work for the big AI companies.

My advice

Apply to both: Micro1 for the number of roles, Mercor for speed. On Mercor, take as many assessments and mini certifications as you can.

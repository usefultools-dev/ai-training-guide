Remote AI Training Jobs for Attorneys

AI companies pay attorneys to help train their models, remotely and on your own schedule. I work on both platforms (I'm not an attorney, so I can speak to the process, not the legal work).

What the work involves

Legal roles typically involve reviewing AI answers to legal questions, writing realistic legal scenarios, and judging whether an output meets professional standards. Advertised rates for attorney roles are often $100+/hr; check each posting, as it varies by practice area and experience.

How to get in

  • Both platforms start with an AI interview. Mercor's felt slightly easier to me. See how to prepare.
  • On Mercor, take as many assessments as you can. Many offers come without an interview. See Mercor assessments.
  • Apply to both. Mercor got me into a role faster; Micro1 has more roles, added daily.
